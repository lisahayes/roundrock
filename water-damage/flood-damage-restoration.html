Why This City Sees Flooding the Way It Does

Central Texas is widely known as Flash Flood Alley, an area recognized as the most flash-flood-prone region in the country, and Round Rock's position along Brushy Creek puts it squarely in that pattern. Intense, fast-moving storms can drop several inches of rain in a matter of hours, and the terrain doesn't always drain quickly enough to keep up.

What Happened Along Brushy Creek in July 2025

Thunderstorms across Central Texas brought three to four inches of rain over just a few days in early July 2025, and the low-water crossing at North Lee Street and Summit Street in central Round Rock closed for several days as Brushy Creek rose. Williamson County's judge declared a local state of disaster in response, and swift water rescue teams were active in the broader region during the event.

The First Steps After Floodwater Enters a Property

Floodwater is treated as contaminated from the moment it enters a structure, regardless of how clean it looked outside, since it typically carries soil, chemicals, and other contaminants from the surrounding area. Extraction and disinfection begin together rather than as separate phases, and porous materials that were submerged usually need to be removed rather than dried in place.

Working With Flood Insurance Versus Standard Coverage

Standard homeowners insurance typically excludes flood damage from rising water, which means a separate flood insurance policy is usually what actually covers a Brushy Creek overflow event. We document flood-related damage specifically for that type of claim and can walk through what we're seeing with your flood insurance provider directly.

Why Extended Drying Time Is Normal After a Flood

A flood-affected space often takes longer to reach a safe moisture baseline than a contained pipe leak would, particularly where water sat for an extended period before extraction crews could safely access the property. We check for secondary issues throughout that extended window, efflorescence on concrete, early staining that suggests mold is already establishing, rather than waiting until a final walkthrough to look for them.

When flooding affects several properties along the same stretch of Brushy Creek, which happens during a significant storm, we can often coordinate scheduling across neighboring properties to make the most efficient use of equipment and crew time for everyone involved.

Flood Damage Restoration Questions From Round Rock Homeowners

Does my regular homeowners policy cover Brushy Creek flooding?

Usually not. Standard homeowners insurance typically excludes damage from rising floodwater, which is why a separate flood insurance policy through the National Flood Insurance Program or a private carrier is what actually applies in most Brushy Creek overflow situations.

How soon can you respond during an active flood event?

We prioritize flood-related calls given how quickly conditions can change, though access to some properties may be limited by road closures during an active event like the July 2025 flooding along Brushy Creek. We'll get out as soon as it's safe to do so.

Is everything that got wet during a flood a total loss?

Not necessarily, but floodwater's contamination level means more gets removed than with a clean water event. We assess material by material during the initial visit rather than assuming everything needs replacement.
