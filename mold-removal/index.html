Mold Removal in Round Rock, TX

Round Rock's hard water and its stock of aging 1990s copper plumbing mean mold calls here trace back to a specific, findable source more often than not.

📞 (512) 764-6513

Where DIY Cleanup Stops Making Sense

A spot roughly the size of a coin, on a hard, non-porous surface like tile, with an obvious cause, is reasonable to handle yourself with a scrub brush and diluted bleach. Once it's spread onto drywall or insulation, or grown past that size, scrubbing without sealing the room first accomplishes nothing useful, it just pushes spores through the return vent into rooms that were clean a moment earlier.

Why the East and West Sides of Town Fail Differently

The clay soil on Round Rock's east side swells and shrinks with the weather, which stresses slab plumbing until a joint separates, often with little external warning before mold shows up. The limestone karst on the west side lets water travel sideways underground, so a leak's actual source can be well away from where the resulting mold first becomes visible.

The Dell-Era Copper Behind So Many Calls

Forest Creek, Brushy Creek, and Cat Hollow all went up during the late 1980s and 1990s boom, and that original copper plumbing is now thirty to forty years old, aged into the window where pinhole leaks become common. Round Rock's hard water, near 15.2 grains per gallon since the city's 2020 shift to Lake Travis supply, accelerates the corrosion that eventually causes those pinholes to fail.

Walking Through an Actual Removal Job

Sealed plastic sheeting goes up first, held under negative air pressure to isolate the rest of the house from demolition. Drywall, insulation, and padding with active growth get removed whole rather than treated in place, since surface cleaning can't undo what's already worked into the material. Framing then gets HEPA-vacuumed, treated with an antimicrobial, and inspected once every trace of visible growth is gone.

What Actually Prevents a Second Visit

A final HEPA pass covers the entire containment zone, not just the spot that looked affected, since demolition pushes spores further than the visible footprint suggests. Then comes the step that's easy to skip, identifying what caused it in the first place, a slab leak, an attic vent routed the wrong way, or grading sloping toward the foundation. Skip that conversation and the job has a real chance of resurfacing within a year.

Weekend and After-Hours Availability

Mold tied to active water, a fresh leak, a burst line, doesn't wait for business hours, so we keep weekend and evening slots open for anything involving standing moisture. Routine inspections and scheduled removal work generally book during normal weekday hours instead.

What That Initial Call Is Really For

When you first call about suspected mold, expect us to ask about what you're seeing, any recent water event, and roughly how long it's been noticeable, enough to schedule the right kind of visit rather than guessing at scope sight unseen.

Same-Day Response When It's Warranted

Most mold calls aren't true emergencies, but an active, spreading situation tied to standing water or a burst pipe still actively running gets treated differently. In those cases we prioritize same-day dispatch, since active water dramatically accelerates how fast a small problem becomes a large one.

What That Initial Call Is Really For

That first phone call is mostly about scoping the visit correctly, we'll ask what you're seeing, whether there's been a recent water issue, and roughly how long it's been going on, so the technician who shows up already has the right equipment for what's likely involved.

Mold Removal Questions From Round Rock Homeowners

Already scrubbed it with bleach and it's still coming back. What's going on?

Bleach never reaches past the surface, so whatever had already worked its way into drywall or wood underneath is completely unaffected by it. It's also worth considering that the moisture behind it, frequently a slab leak in this city, might still be actively running.

What actually confirms a slab leak is the real cause?

The shape of the growth itself is usually the giveaway, clustered tightly in one spot rather than scattered, and when that lines up with a water bill that's crept up for no obvious reason, we're usually confident before detection equipment ever comes out.

Does it matter which side of Round Rock my house is on?

It can. Homes on the west side's limestone karst sometimes have growth appear away from the actual leak source, while homes on the east side's clay soil tend to see more direct joint failures tied to the ground's constant movement.

Bigger than a coin, or already on drywall? Set the scrub brush down and call instead.
